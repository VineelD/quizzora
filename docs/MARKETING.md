# Quizzora marketing playbook

Concise guide for growing Quizzora without ads or complex features. Focus on trust, curriculum fit, and word-of-mouth.

**Live site:** [https://quizzora.org](https://quizzora.org)  
**Source:** [https://github.com/VineelD/quizzora](https://github.com/VineelD/quizzora)

---

## Core messages

### Schools

- **Curriculum-aligned practice** — Years 7–12 including VCE; teachers pick ACARA-mapped topics and assign AI-generated quizzes to classes.
- **Study Coach before the quiz** — assignment-scoped coaching with integrity guardrails; not a generic chatbot.
- **Australian data residency** — hosted service runs on-premises in Australia; see [School DPA](SCHOOL-DPA.md).
- **Transparent software** — AGPL-3.0 source; inspect, self-host, or contribute. Subscriptions fund hosting and development, not a paywall on code.
- **Low-friction trial** — free trial with card; demo available for leadership teams via [support@quizzora.org](mailto:support@quizzora.org).

### Families / homeschool

- **Same curriculum depth as schools** — one family workspace for parents and children.
- **Focused student portal** — no distractions; guardian progress links.
- **Privacy-first** — Australian-hosted; family data isolated by join code.
- **Affordable alternative** — open source means you can self-host; hosted plans fund the shared platform.

## 90-day “easy first” checklist

Do these in order. Each block is designed to fit a week; skip nothing in Week 1.

### Week 1 — foundations (this week)

- [ ] Polish [README](../README.md) and ensure [quizzora.org](https://quizzora.org) is linked from GitHub repo settings (Website field).
- [ ] Post one LinkedIn update: what Quizzora is, link to live site + GitHub, one screenshot or classroom image.
- [ ] Email 3 teachers or coordinators you know: offer a 15-minute demo, link to `/pricing`.
- [ ] Add Quizzora to your email signature: tagline + [quizzora.org](https://quizzora.org).

### Weeks 2–4 — credibility

- [ ] Share [competitive positioning](COMPETITIVE-POSITIONING.md) internally; use honest strengths (Study Coach, AU curriculum, AGPL) in conversations — not “better than EP.”
- [ ] Offer one free pilot school a guided onboarding (you run the first quiz creation with them).
- [ ] Publish a short GitHub release note when you ship something visible (even small UX fixes).
- [ ] Ask one happy user for a one-sentence quote you can reuse on the site or LinkedIn.

### Weeks 5–8 — reach

- [ ] Second LinkedIn post: “why open source for schools” — link [OPEN-SOURCE.md](OPEN-SOURCE.md).
- [ ] Reach out to one Australian ed-tech or teacher Facebook group (helpful post, not spam).
- [ ] List Quizzora on relevant open-source education directories (Awesome lists, GitHub topics: `australian-curriculum`, `edtech`).
- [ ] Send [School DPA](SCHOOL-DPA.md) proactively when a school asks about privacy.

### Weeks 9–12 — repeat what works

- [ ] Double down on the channel that produced the most signups (usually word-of-mouth or LinkedIn).
- [ ] One more demo offer to a school leadership team.
- [ ] Review pricing page copy and homepage — tiny tweaks only; no redesign.
- [ ] Document one “how we use Quizzora” story (teacher workflow, 5 bullets).

## Channels

| Channel | What to do | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GitHub** | Clear README, issues welcome, link to docs | Developers and technically minded schools trust inspectable code |
| **LinkedIn** | Monthly posts: mission, curriculum, open source, demos | Reaches Australian educators and school leaders |
| **Teacher word-of-mouth** | Pilots, demos, ask for referrals after a successful trial | Highest conversion; aligns with school buying cycles |

---

## What NOT to do early

- Paid ads (Google, Meta, LinkedIn sponsored) — expensive before product-market fit.
- Referral programs or affiliate codes — engineering cost; premature optimization.
- Analytics pixels / heavy tracking — privacy story matters; use server logs or simple uptime checks instead.
- Big redesign or new features for marketing — polish copy and trust signals first.
- Overclaiming vs Education Perfect / Atomi — use [competitive positioning](COMPETITIVE-POSITIONING.md) honestly.
- Spamming teacher groups or cold lists — one helpful post beats ten pitches.
